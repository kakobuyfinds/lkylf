How to check a find before ordering

Check the original listing

Open the product card and look for the original seller link. If you plan to use KakoBuy, check whether it accepts that link and carries your size and color. Save the seller’s measurements and item ID before ordering.

Review warehouse photos

If your agent provides quality-control (QC) photos, compare the received color, labels and measurements with your order. For a hoodie, ask for a tape-measure photo across the chest and compare it with a hoodie you own.

Compare shipping quotes

Use the same destination, parcel weight and dimensions when comparing lines. Ask which weight the quote uses and whether it includes taxes or extra charges. Check the return window before approving shipment.

Estimate the delivered cost

Enter your quotes in CNY. Convert any fees quoted in another currency before adding them.

Blank fees count as zero in the estimate. Check each fee before paying.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each card opens its HipobuyFind listing. Both main links open the HipobuyFind homepage.

Can I use these listings with KakoBuy?

Open a listing and find its original marketplace URL. Paste that URL into KakoBuy to see whether the item and your variant can be ordered.

Why is the item price lower than my delivered total?

The displayed price is for the item. Depending on the order, you may also pay for delivery to the warehouse, agent or payment fees, international shipping, and duties or taxes.

What should I do if a link or variant is unavailable?

Search by the item name or browse the same category on HipobuyFind. Compare the new seller’s photos, size chart and return terms before choosing a replacement.
